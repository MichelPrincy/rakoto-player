Brume des Hautes Terres — RAKOTO Player website ambience

Original procedural composition and sound design created for this project.
Sine/triangle synthesis, 76 BPM, with no recordings, third-party songs,
samples, streaming services or microphone input.

The generated ambience is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The website starts silently. Sound requires a deliberate button press.
The visualizer analyses the site's generated audio only when it is active.
Otherwise its static shape is explicitly identified as decorative.

Window and equalizer images: actual RAKOTO Player Windows application,
captured with demonstration data. Android and iOS device compositions
are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots of released mobile interfaces.
womanheadphone.png: supplied by the project owner; optimized copies used
without changing the subject. Original logo supplied by the owner.
